§ 04 — TECHNIQUES

How we
built it

01

Vertex AI RAG Engine + Discovery Engine

Two-stage retrieval pipeline. Vertex AI RAG Engine ingests PDFs into managed vector stores. Production retrieval uses Discovery Engine Search API for robust unstructured parsing, advanced OCR on tabular documents, and extractive segments.

02

Gemini Pro synthesis + Flash adversarial judge

Gemini 1.5 Pro synthesises the answer from ranked chunks. The answer is then passed to Gemini Flash acting as an adversarial evaluator, returning a structured verdict with faithfulness score, citation accuracy, and concrete counter-arguments.

03

Session-level merkle provenance chaining

Every interaction produces a provenance hash chained to the previous hash in the session. Any tampering with a single past answer breaks every subsequent hash. The session becomes a tamper-evident audit trail.

04

Modal serverless persistence ledger

All persistence stored on Modal, not a database. Five volumes (NDJSON append-only) and two dicts constitute the entire data layer. The append-only pattern gives cryptographic reproducibility at zero idle cost.
