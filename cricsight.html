§ 01 — Intelligence Layer

Beyond the scoreboard.
Into the pressure.

Standard cricket analytics show you what happened. CricSight Powerplay shows you why it felt inevitable — the mounting pressure of dot balls, the momentum shift after a dropped catch, the over where the match was actually lost.

Built for Bangladesh Premier League data because BPL is exactly where this intelligence matters most and is least available. T20 cricket is a pressure game. The analysis should reflect that.

∫
Pressure Index

Over-by-over cumulative pressure calculated from required run rate delta, wicket context, and batting position. The number that explains the scorecard.

P
Win Probability Curves

Ball-by-ball win probability updated continuously. Shows not just who won, but the exact moment the match tipped — and by how much.

∑
Player Performance Embeddings

Vector representations of player performance across context-matched situations. Who performs under pressure. Who fades.

The orchestration

Three models.
One match query.
Each doing the job it's best at.

Gemini 2.5 Pro

Deep match analysis. The long-form reasoning. Tournament context and tactical narrative.

Gemini Flash

Over-by-over narration. Fast, contextual, structured. The live commentary layer.

LangGraph

Routes queries between models based on complexity and context. The orchestration layer that makes the system coherent.

§ 02 — Technical Stack

What's under
the scoreboard.

LangGraph

Multi-model orchestration routing queries between Gemini 2.5 Pro for deep analysis, Flash for narration, and Claude 3.5 Sonnet for editorial card writing. Task routing based on query complexity and context depth required.

Gemini 2.5 Pro

Deep match analysis, tournament context, tactical narrative generation. The reasoning model for questions that require thinking about the whole game, not just the over.

Cricsheet JSON

Ball-by-ball match data for Bangladesh Premier League. Every delivery, every wicket, every over — parsed into pressure and probability calculations.

Next.js + Framer Motion

Editorial, linen-palette aesthetic. Slot-machine headline animations, animated architecture dropdowns, embedded data visualizations. Bangladesh green as the sole accent color.

BigQuery

Match data warehouse for historical BPL analysis. Aggregated statistics, player performance vectors, tournament-level context queries.
